The Humanities and Interpretive Social Sciences Research Hub runs a programme of talks, book launches, screenings and workshops across the year, alongside its research clusters and twice-yearly whole-group Conversations. Events listed here are drawn automatically from the Hub's shared calendar, so what you see is what the Hub has scheduled.

For the Hub itself, its clusters and its people, visit [hiss.utsc.utoronto.ca](https://hiss.utsc.utoronto.ca).
