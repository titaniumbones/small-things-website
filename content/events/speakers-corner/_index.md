A new series from the Department of Historical and Cultural Studies: fifteen-minute talks by faculty on anything they think students would find worth knowing. It need not be their research. The format takes its cue from Shawkat Toorawa's [Dr. T project](https://lynnborton.com/2025/07/10/the-dr-t-project-things-worth-knowing-with-shawkat-toorawa/), whose motto is "dare to be curious".
