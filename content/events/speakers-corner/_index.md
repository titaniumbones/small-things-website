Speakers' Corner is a new series from the Department of Historical and Cultural Studies. Each episode is a fifteen-minute talk by a faculty member on anything they think students would find worth knowing. It need not be their research. A legendary sports rivalry, a sitcom, a forgotten piece of Scarborough history, a word with an odd past: the only rule is that the speaker finds it interesting and thinks you might too.

The format takes its cue from Shawkat Toorawa's [Dr. T project](https://lynnborton.com/2025/07/10/the-dr-t-project-things-worth-knowing-with-shawkat-toorawa/), whose motto is "dare to be curious".

Speakers' Corner is organised within HCS. Dates for the first season will appear here as they are confirmed.
