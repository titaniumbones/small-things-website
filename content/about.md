  - label: "The series"
    body: |
      *Small Things* is a live-recorded conversation series. Each episode, host Matt Price sits down with a faculty member from one of the Hub's six founding departments and talks about one object. A coin, a photograph, a recipe, a tool, a word: something small enough to hold, chosen because it opens onto a much larger question about culture, history or society.

      Recordings happen in front of a live audience on campus, usually over lunch, with pizza. Students are welcome, and the series is meant as a doorway: come for the object, stay to find out what research in the humanities looks like up close, and how to get involved in it.
  - label: "People"
    body: |
      **Matt Price** hosts the series. He teaches in Historical and Cultural Studies and is the HISS Hub's faculty lead.

      Episodes are edited and published by UTSC students in paid post-production roles.
  - label: "The HISS Research Hub"
    body: |
      The Hub brings together faculty from six departments at UTSC:

      - [Anthropology](https://www.utsc.utoronto.ca/anthropology/)
      - [Arts, Culture and Media](https://www.utsc.utoronto.ca/acm/)
      - [English](https://www.utsc.utoronto.ca/engdept/)
      - [Geography](https://www.utsc.utoronto.ca/geography/)
      - [Historical and Cultural Studies](https://www.utsc.utoronto.ca/hcs/)
      - [Language Studies](https://www.utsc.utoronto.ca/dls/)

      It runs research clusters, whole-group Conversations events, and a talk series across the year.
  - label: "Funding"
    body: |
      *Small Things* is supported by a Research for Inclusive Excellence (RIE) grant from the Office of the Vice-Principal Research and Innovation at UTSC, with additional support from the six founding departments of the HISS Hub. The Hub's mentoring partnerships are funded by the Mentoring Partnership Fund.
