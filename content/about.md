  - label: "The series"
    body: |
      Each episode, host Matt Price sits down with a faculty member from one of the Hub's six founding departments and talks about one object. A coin, a photograph, a recipe, a tool, a word: something small enough to hold that opens onto a much larger question.
  - label: "People"
    body: |
      **Matt Price** hosts. He teaches in Historical and Cultural Studies and is the HISS Hub's faculty lead. Episodes are edited and published by UTSC students in paid post-production roles.
  - label: "The HISS Research Hub"
    body: |
      Six departments at UTSC:

      - [Anthropology](https://www.utsc.utoronto.ca/anthropology/)
      - [Arts, Culture and Media](https://www.utsc.utoronto.ca/acm/)
      - [English](https://www.utsc.utoronto.ca/engdept/)
      - [Geography](https://www.utsc.utoronto.ca/geography/)
      - [Historical and Cultural Studies](https://www.utsc.utoronto.ca/hcs/)
      - [Language Studies](https://www.utsc.utoronto.ca/dls/)
  - label: "Funding"
    body: |
      A Research for Inclusive Excellence grant from the Office of the Vice-Principal Research and Innovation at UTSC, with support from the Hub's six founding departments.
