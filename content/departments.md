## Anthropology

Anthropology studies human life in all its range, from the deep past to the present, through fieldwork, archaeology and the study of human biology. [Department of Anthropology](https://www.utsc.utoronto.ca/anthropology/)

## Arts, Culture and Media

Arts, Culture and Media brings together art history, studio art, music, theatre and performance, and media studies, joining the making of art with the study of it. [Department of Arts, Culture and Media](https://www.utsc.utoronto.ca/acm/)

## English

English studies literature, film and other cultural forms in English, across periods and places, along with creative writing. [Department of English](https://www.utsc.utoronto.ca/engdept/)

## Human Geography

Human Geography studies how people make and live in places, from cities and environments to migration and economies, with a strong focus on Scarborough and the Toronto region. [Department of Human Geography](https://www.utsc.utoronto.ca/geography/)

## Historical and Cultural Studies

Historical and Cultural Studies brings together history, religion, classics, women's and gender studies, and African, Caribbean and diaspora studies, among others, to study the past and how cultures understand it. [Department of Historical and Cultural Studies](https://www.utsc.utoronto.ca/hcs/)

## Language Studies

Language Studies teaches and researches languages and linguistics, including French, Chinese, Tamil and other languages of Scarborough's communities. [Department of Language Studies](https://www.utsc.utoronto.ca/dls/)
