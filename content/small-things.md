One object. One guest. One hour. *Small Things* is the HISS Research Hub's live-recorded podcast.

## What it is

Every episode starts with one object on the table. A guest from one of the Hub's six departments brings something they have been thinking about, and for an hour we follow it wherever it leads: into an archive, across an ocean, back a few centuries, or around the corner into a question nobody had thought to ask.

Recordings are live, on campus, at lunchtime, with pizza. Anyone can come. UTSC students in paid post-production roles edit each recording into a finished episode.

## Be a guest

Faculty members: we are looking for guests. Bring one object you keep coming back to. No slides, no paper. Most of the work is ours.

1. **Propose an object.** Email [Matt Price](mailto:matt.price@utoronto.ca?subject=Small%20Things%3A%20an%20object) with the object and a line on where it leads. No abstract, no CV.
2. **Find the date.** A short conversation to settle a lunchtime that works and sketch the questions worth asking.
3. **Record live over lunch.** One hour in KW 130 at UTSC, 1:00 to 2:30 pm, in front of a live audience of students and colleagues. Pizza is on us.
4. **Students edit.** UTSC students in paid post-production roles edit the recording into a finished episode.
5. **It goes out.** Published here and on the usual podcast apps, with the object front and centre.

## Episodes

Recordings are on the Hub's calendar as they are scheduled. The 2026–27 season plans six to eight recordings; dates to be announced.
